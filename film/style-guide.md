# KAIRO — visual style guide

## Format

16:9, 1920x1080 (rendered from a 3840x2160 master), 24 fps, 300 s, no
subtitles burned in, no watermark, no logo.

## Look

Cinematic 2D stickman animation. The characters are flat ink-and-fill stick
figures; the worlds behind them are painterly layered backgrounds with
atmospheric depth. The contrast between the simple figure and the rich
environment is the signature of the series.

- Line: warm black ink `#181412`, no gradients on the character.
- Fill: paper skin `#E8DDCB`, hide brown `#7A5634`, suit `#18181B`.
- Rim light: warm `#FFD08A` from the practical source (lamp, sun, map glow),
  always on the lit side only — never a full halo.
- Backgrounds: 4–5 value planes, haze between planes, vignette 0.45–0.6.
- Grain: subtle (σ ≈ 4–5), never digital-clean.

## Camera language

| Beat | Move |
|---|---|
| Narration in the room | very slow push-in, no shake |
| Memory / savanna reveal | crane down, then ground level |
| Hunt | lateral tracking |
| Chase | lateral track → over-the-shoulder, slight shake |
| Cave | locked frames, then push into darkness |
| Map | macro glide along the engravings |
| Revelation | match cut, push to face, fade to black |

Every shot is exactly 12 seconds and contains **no cut inside the shot** — the
cut is always between scenes.

## Palette by act

| Act | Key colours |
|---|---|
| Present | `#0C0B0D` room, `#CE9E5C` lamp, `#DED2BC` clock face |
| Savanna | `#E8B276` horizon, `#C49E5C` grass, `#303A22` acacia, `#565052` hills |
| Cave | `#0A0A0C` shadow, `#7A6E64` rock, `#78C4C8` cold bounce |
| Map | `#C6985E` amber stone, `#FFD68A` glow core, `#8EE0FF` cool edge |

## Sound

Score is original and synthesised for this episode: warm pad and clock tick in
the room, wind and wildlife in the savanna, low percussion in the chase, a sub
drone and water drips in the cave, restrained chimes for the map, and a single
dramatic accent at 04:52 before the fade. The narration always sits on top;
the bed never masks the Persian.

## Do not

- No 3D, no photoreal humans, no live action, no mixed styles.
- No text, captions, watermarks or logos in frame.
- No redesign of KAIRO, the predator, the cave or the map between shots.
- No unexplained teleporting, no lighting flips inside a scene.
